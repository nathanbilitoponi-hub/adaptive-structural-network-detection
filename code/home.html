Urban Infrastructure

Structural Network Engine can ingest noisy urban spatial data and extract the dominant structural backbone of the city.

From fragmented road-like point clouds or spatial observations, the engine identifies the main structural corridors, secondary branches and critical junctions.

This allows the compression of complex urban networks into a structural backbone useful for infrastructure analysis, smart city planning and geospatial intelligence.
